Executive Architecture Summary: TrustForge is an automated compliance auditing and model benchmarking platform designed to evaluate enterprise data privacy posture against India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. This document delivers complete visual evidence of all customer-facing workflows, the complete 14-step statutory assessment wizard, empirical analytics suites (hybrid RAG with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and TOPSIS MCDM), and the isolated Administrative Governance Console. Every figure represents active application execution verified under strict continuous integration and zero-paid commercial inference boundary enforcement.

Customer Platform & Identity Workflows

Figure 1: TrustForge Public Landing Page & Privacy Assurance Hero

Route: / (Public) Role: Public Visitor

The landing page provides the primary interface for organizations seeking automated self-assessment against the DPDP Act 2023. It highlights the platform's core architectural components: hybrid RAG retrieval, multi-model benchmarking, and evidence management. Visitors can initiate a non-authenticated preliminary domain scan or proceed to create an enterprise account.

Figure 2: Instant Public Domain Assessment & Scanner

Route: /scan (Public) Role: Public Visitor

Executes an unauthenticated, non-invasive perimeter scan of a target web domain to check public privacy configurations. It checks for the presence of cookie banners, privacy policy links, SSL/TLS transport availability, and detectable third-party scripts. The resulting summary identifies observable perimeter issues and provides recommendations for full compliance auditing.

Figure 3: Customer Authentication Portal

Route: /login (Public/Client) Role: Client User / Compliance Lead

Implements multi-tenant credential authentication using standard PBKDF2 password hashing (SHA-512, 10,000 iterations) with unique salt. Upon successful authentication, the backend issues an HMAC-SHA256 session token with tenant organization claims. Tenant-scoped route middleware ensures users cannot access or view data belonging to other registered organizations.

Figure 4: Enterprise Registration & Account Creation

Route: /signup (Public) Role: Prospective Client

Allows new enterprise compliance personnel to register a tenant account with email validation and password complexity checks. Generates a dedicated organization identifier and provisions initial tenant records in the durable application store. Upon registration, the user is transitioned into the onboarding workflow to configure initial organizational parameters.

Figure 5: Enterprise Organization Profile Setup Wizard

Route: /onboarding (Authenticated Client) Role: Organization Administrator

Collects organizational metadata including corporate entity name, industry sector, primary website domain, and designated DPO details. Determines whether the entity self-classifies as a Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) under DPDP Act Section 10 obligations. Initializes default assessment baselines and generates domain verification tokens for ownership validation.

Figure 6: Executive Privacy Compliance Dashboard

Route: /dashboard (Authenticated Client) Role: Compliance Officer / DPO

Displays the tenant's current Trust Score, assessment completion progress, active findings count, and evidence metrics. Summary cards categorize compliance observations across the six statutory pillars and highlight open remediation items. Provides direct links to start or resume the 14-step assessment, trigger perimeter scans, and export summary reports.

Figure 7: Organization Profile & Data Fiduciary Configuration

Route: /settings/organization (Client Admin) Role: Organization Administrator

Allows administrators to update organizational records, designated Data Protection Officer contact details, and corporate headquarters. Enables maintenance of corporate sector classifications and review of applicable DPDP Act statutory requirements. All profile updates are recorded in the organization audit history with timestamps and user identifiers.

Figure 8: Automated DNS TXT Domain Ownership Verification

Route: /settings/organization#domain-verification Role: Organization Administrator

Generates a unique DNS TXT challenge token formatted as `trustforge-site-verification=` to verify domain ownership. The backend performs asynchronous DNS lookups against public nameservers to confirm token propagation prior to active scanning. Ensures website scans and compliance scoring are only initiated for domains legitimately managed by the tenant organization.

14-Step Statutory Assessment Wizard

Figure 9: Assessment Step 1 — Governance & Accountability Architecture

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-1 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates organizational data governance mechanisms, internal compliance reporting hierarchies, and DPO appointments. Maps directly to DPDP Act Section 8(1) and Section 10(2)(a) obligations for responsible data fiduciary governance. Audits formal documentation, board-level oversight policies, and designated privacy officer operational roles.

Figure 10: Assessment Step 2 — Consent Architecture & Validation

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-2 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits affirmative, freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent mechanisms under DPDP Act Section 6(1). Checks that opt-in selections are unchecked by default, bundled terms are avoided, and withdrawal procedures are accessible. Verifies timestamped consent recordkeeping and specific purpose declarations for each data processing activity.

Figure 11: Assessment Step 3 — Multilingual Statutory Notice Delivery

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-3 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates statutory privacy notice completeness, itemization of collected personal data, and DPO contact details under Section 5. Checks support for notice availability in English and the 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India. Verifies clear instructions on how data principals may exercise rights or lodge grievances with the fiduciary.

Figure 12: Assessment Step 4 — Consent Manager Integration & Interoperability

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-4 Role: Compliance Assessor

Assesses technical readiness for integrating with Data Protection Board-registered Consent Managers under Section 6(7)-(9). Evaluates API interfaces and workflows enabling data principals to view, manage, and withdraw consent through unified mechanisms. Audits procedures for propagating consent revocation across internal databases and third-party data processors.

Figure 13: Assessment Step 5 — Purpose Limitation & Data Minimization

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-5 Role: Compliance Assessor

Assesses adherence to statutory collection boundaries under DPDP Act Section 4 and Section 7, ensuring only necessary data is collected. Identifies potential collection creep, collateral data gathering, and secondary use beyond the consented purpose. Checks safeguards preventing personal data collected for transactional purposes from being reused for unauthorized profiling.

Figure 14: Assessment Step 6 — Storage Limitation & Secure Erasure

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-6 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates defined data retention schedules, automated archival triggers, and secure deletion protocols under Section 8(7). Verifies that personal data is deleted once the specified processing purpose is accomplished or consent is formally revoked. Audits tombstone tracking records, deletion verification logs, and legal hold override controls.

Figure 15: Assessment Step 7 — Data Quality, Completeness & Accuracy

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-7 Role: Compliance Assessor

Assesses mechanisms for maintaining data accuracy and completeness when personal data is used to make decisions affecting data principals. Maps directly to DPDP Act Section 8(3) obligations requiring reasonable measures to ensure data consistency. Reviews user self-service correction interfaces, synchronization processes, and downstream processor notification workflows.

Figure 16: Assessment Step 8 — Reasonable Technical & Security Safeguards

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-8 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits technical security controls including TLS encryption in transit, role-based access control, and credential safeguards. Evaluates organizational adherence to DPDP Act Section 8(5) requiring reasonable security safeguards to prevent data breaches. Reviews vulnerability review cadences, system patching policies, and multi-factor authentication enforcement.

Figure 17: Assessment Step 9 — Personal Data Breach Incident Management

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-9 Role: Compliance Assessor

Reviews incident response procedures and formal breach reporting plans as required under DPDP Act Section 8(6). Evaluates playbooks for notifying the Data Protection Board of India and affected data principals upon discovering a personal data breach. Checks incident containment steps, forensic audit logging mechanisms, and statutory reporting escalation timelines.

Figure 18: Assessment Step 10 — Children's Data & Persons with Disabilities

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-10 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates parental consent verification protocols and strict prohibitions against tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising to minors. Enforces compliance with DPDP Act Section 9 mandates protecting children and persons under legal guardianship. Reviews age-verification workflows, neutral gating methods, and absence of harmful behavioral monitoring practices.

Figure 19: Assessment Step 11 — Data Principal Rights Redressal Architecture

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-11 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits self-service procedures for processing data principal requests: right to access (Sec 11), correction (Sec 12), and erasure. Evaluates identity verification methods, data export formats, and response tracking against statutory fulfillment windows. Reviews mechanisms supporting the right of nomination in the event of death or legal incapacity under Section 14.

Figure 20: Assessment Step 12 — Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Timelines

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-12 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates internal grievance redressal procedures and Data Protection Officer escalation channels under Section 13. Verifies ticket logging systems that record complaints and track resolution turnaround times. Checks that communications inform data principals of their right to appeal unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board.

Figure 21: Assessment Step 13 — Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) Obligations

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-13 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits higher-tier obligations applicable to designated Significant Data Fiduciaries under DPDP Act Section 10. Assesses periodic Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) frameworks, independent audit readiness, and resident DPO appointments. Reviews algorithmic risk monitoring procedures and board reporting cadences for systemic privacy impacts.

Figure 22: Assessment Step 14 — Final Audit Review & Evidence Synthesis

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-14 Role: Compliance Assessor

Consolidates responses from all 14 statutory control sections into a pre-submission review matrix with completeness indicators. Allows the compliance assessor to inspect consistency, attach supplementary documentation, and confirm submission accuracy. Final submission locks the assessment version and initiates deterministic scoring and RAG citation validation.

Figure 23: Complete Statutory Compliance Evaluation Results

Route: /assessment/results/:id Role: Compliance Officer / DPO

Presents the completed audit scorecard, section-by-section compliance percentages, and identified statutory gap summaries. Categorizes compliance gaps by severity (High, Medium, Low) based on statutory obligations and potential exposure tiers. Generates actionable remediation recommendations and provides access to printable summary reports.
