Executive Architecture Summary: TrustForge is an autonomous compliance engineering platform built to audit, benchmark, and certify enterprise compliance under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. This document delivers complete, verifiable visual evidence of all customer-facing workflows, the complete 14-step statutory assessment wizard, algorithmic analytics suites (RRF RAG and TOPSIS MCDM), and the isolated Administrative Governance Console. Every figure represents active execution verified under strict continuous integration and zero-paid-inference boundary enforcement.

Customer Platform & Identity Workflows

Figure 1: TrustForge Public Landing Page & Privacy Assurance Hero

Route: / (Public) Role: Public Visitor

The landing page provides the primary entry point for enterprises seeking autonomous DPDP Act 2023 compliance auditing. It showcases the core value propositions: deterministic RAG analysis, empirical model benchmarking, and automated evidence management. Visitors can immediately trigger an unauthenticated preliminary website scan or proceed to secure enterprise onboarding.

Figure 2: Instant Public Domain Assessment & Scanner

Route: /scan (Public) Role: Public Visitor

Provides an instant non-invasive perimeter privacy scan for any corporate domain without requiring upfront registration. It tests cookie consent banners, privacy policy accessibility, SSL/TLS transport security, and public trackers. The resulting scorecard generates immediate actionable findings and invites the user to create an account for full remediation.

Figure 3: Secure Customer Authentication Portal

Route: /login (Public/Client) Role: Client User / Data Protection Officer

Implements multi-tenant credential authentication with bcrypt hashing, strict rate-limiting, and timing-safe password validation. Customer credentials are strictly segmented to prevent cross-tenant identity leaks or privilege escalation. Upon successful authentication, the backend issues an HMAC-signed JWT token enforcing organization boundary filters.

Figure 4: Enterprise Registration & Account Creation

Route: /signup (Public) Role: Prospective Client

Allows new enterprise compliance teams and Data Protection Officers to register a dedicated tenant account. Validates organizational email domains, enforces strong password complexity criteria, and provisions isolated database records. New signups are automatically transitioned into the tenant onboarding wizard to establish baseline organization parameters.

Figure 5: Enterprise Organization Profile Setup Wizard

Route: /onboarding (Authenticated Client) Role: Organization Administrator

Captures essential corporate metadata including legal corporate name, industry sector, registered domain, and data fiduciary tier. Determines whether the entity qualifies as a Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) under DPDP Act Section 10 obligations. Initializes default compliance posture baselines and prepares the organization workspace for domain ownership verification.

Figure 6: Executive Privacy Compliance Dashboard

Route: /dashboard (Authenticated Client) Role: Compliance Officer / DPO

Delivers an executive overview of the tenant's real-time Trust Score, assessment status, active findings, and evidence metrics. Visual cards highlight critical compliance vulnerabilities, pending remediation tasks, and upcoming statutory filing deadlines. Provides one-click navigation to the 14-step assessment, website scanner, evidence vault, and formal statutory export suites.

Figure 7: Organization Profile & Data Fiduciary Configuration

Route: /settings/organization (Client Admin) Role: Organization Administrator

Enables configuration of tenant legal metadata, Data Protection Officer contact details, and corporate headquarters location. Allows administrators to designate enterprise data processing sectors and review data fiduciary classification status. Changes are immediately audited and tied to the organization's immutable audit log trail for governance compliance.

Figure 8: Automated DNS TXT Domain Ownership Verification

Route: /settings/organization#domain-verification Role: Organization Administrator

Generates a cryptographically unique DNS TXT challenge token (`trustforge-site-verification=...`) to prove enterprise domain ownership. The backend performs asynchronous DNS lookups against authorative name servers to validate the token before enabling active domain scanning. Prevents unauthorized third parties from scanning, scraping, or claiming corporate domains without explicit DNS administrative authority.

14-Step Statutory Assessment Wizard

Figure 9: Assessment Step 1 — Governance & Accountability Architecture

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-1 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates organizational data governance structure, Data Protection Officer appointment, and internal compliance charters. Maps directly to DPDP Act Section 8(1) and Section 10(2)(a) requirements for accountable privacy governance frameworks. Assesses formal documentation, reporting lines, and board-level oversight for organizational data processing activities.

Figure 10: Assessment Step 2 — Consent Architecture & Validation

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-2 Role: Compliance Assessor

Inspects affirmative, freely given, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent mechanisms under DPDP Act Section 6(1). Verifies that opt-in checkboxes are unchecked by default, dark patterns are prohibited, and consent records are auditable. Ensures data fiduciaries capture explicit consent timestamps and itemized processing purpose declarations.

Figure 11: Assessment Step 3 — Multilingual Statutory Notice Delivery

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-3 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits statutory privacy notice clarity, itemization of collected personal data, and DPO contact transparency under Section 5. Validates availability of notice in English and all 22 languages specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India. Checks that notices describe data principal rights and the explicit procedure for withdrawing consent or lodging complaints.

Figure 12: Assessment Step 4 — Consent Manager Integration & Interoperability

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-4 Role: Compliance Assessor

Verifies interoperability with Data Protection Board-registered Consent Managers as mandated under Section 6(7)-(9). Assesses API endpoints and protocols that allow data principals to view, manage, and revoke consent through unified portals. Ensures consent lifecycle events (grant, modification, revocation) trigger immediate downstream data processing halts.

Figure 13: Assessment Step 5 — Purpose Limitation & Data Minimization

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-5 Role: Compliance Assessor

Examines adherence to DPDP Act Section 4 and Section 7 processing boundaries, verifying that only strictly necessary data is collected. Flags excessive data collection practices, collateral data gathering, and purpose creep across corporate systems. Validates that personal data collected for a specified transaction is not reused for profiling or automated marketing without separate consent.

Figure 14: Assessment Step 6 — Storage Limitation & Secure Erasure

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-6 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits data retention schedules, automated archival policies, and cryptographic erasure protocols under Section 8(7). Verifies that personal data is deleted as soon as the specified purpose is fulfilled or consent is formally withdrawn. Assesses corporate tombstone tracking, shredding verification logs, and legal hold override safeguards.

Figure 15: Assessment Step 7 — Data Quality, Completeness & Accuracy

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-7 Role: Compliance Assessor

Inspects data lifecycle integrity mechanisms to ensure personal data used to make decisions affecting data principals is accurate. Maps directly to DPDP Act Section 8(3) obligations requiring reasonable efforts to maintain completeness and consistency. Evaluates self-service data correction interfaces, synchronization pipelines, and third-party data processor propagation.

Figure 16: Assessment Step 8 — Reasonable Technical & Security Safeguards

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-8 Role: Compliance Assessor

Verifies mandatory technical security controls including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and role-based access. Audits compliance with DPDP Act Section 8(5) requiring reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches. Checks penetration testing cadences, vulnerability management programs, and multi-factor authentication enforcement.

Figure 17: Assessment Step 9 — Personal Data Breach Incident Management

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-9 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits incident response playbooks and formal breach notification workflows under DPDP Act Section 8(6). Verifies that protocols exist to inform both the Data Protection Board of India and affected data principals without delay. Evaluates containment timelines, forensic logging procedures, and CERT-In coordination mechanisms.

Figure 18: Assessment Step 10 — Children's Data & Persons with Disabilities

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-10 Role: Compliance Assessor

Evaluates verifiable parental consent mechanisms and strict prohibitions against tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising. Enforces strict adherence to DPDP Act Section 9 mandates safeguarding minors and individuals under legal guardianship. Checks age assurance systems, neutral age gating, and absence of harmful behavioral monitoring algorithms.

Figure 19: Assessment Step 11 — Data Principal Rights Redressal Architecture

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-11 Role: Compliance Assessor

Inspects automated self-service workflows for the right to access (Sec 11), right to correction (Sec 12), and right to erasure. Verifies data export portability formats (JSON/CSV), identity verification checks, and 30-day statutory fulfillment timers. Evaluates the right of nomination framework enabling designated representatives in event of death or incapacity.

Figure 20: Assessment Step 12 — Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Timelines

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-12 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits the organizational grievance redressal hierarchy and Data Protection Officer escalation paths under Section 13. Verifies that ticket tracking systems record citizen complaints and enforce statutory resolution turnaround times. Ensures data principals are clearly advised of their right to appeal unresolved grievances to the Data Protection Board.

Figure 21: Assessment Step 13 — Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) Obligations

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-13 Role: Compliance Assessor

Audits specialized institutional requirements for designated Significant Data Fiduciaries under DPDP Act Section 10. Validates periodic Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA), independent statutory audits, and resident DPO appointments. Assesses algorithmic impact audits, systemic risk monitoring, and executive compliance reporting frameworks.

Figure 22: Assessment Step 14 — Final Audit Review & Evidence Synthesis

Route: /assessment/wizard/step-14 Role: Compliance Assessor

Aggregates all 14 statutory control sections into a unified pre-submission audit summary matrix with completeness indicators. Allows compliance auditors to inspect response consistency, link supplementary documentary evidence, and confirm accuracy. Submitting this step freezes the assessment snapshot and triggers real-time RAG scoring and multi-criteria TOPSIS evaluation.

Figure 23: Complete Statutory Compliance Evaluation Results

Route: /assessment/results/:id Role: Compliance Officer / DPO
