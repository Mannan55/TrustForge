## 1. Embedding Model Specification & Provenance

To satisfy academic research rigor, matching vector dimensionality alone ($d = 384$) is insufficient to declare embedding compatibility. Two unrelated embedding models (such as `MiniLM-L6-v2` and `bge-small-en-v1.5`) both produce 384-dimensional vectors but reside in distinct mathematical vector spaces where dot products produce meaningless noise.

This audit confirms that the document embeddings stored in Supabase PostgreSQL and the runtime query embeddings generated by the TrustForge retrieval engine share an identical model, tokenizer, pooling strategy, and normalization standard.

### Model Architecture & Configuration
- **Model Identifier**: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`
- **Underlying Architecture**: 6-layer MiniLM transformer with 12 attention heads
- **Hidden Layer Dimension**: 384 dimensions
- **Tokenizer**: WordPiece vocabulary (30,522 tokens) with uncased normalization
- **Maximum Sequence Length**: 256 tokens (truncated beyond 256)
- **Pooling Strategy**: Mean pooling with attention mask weighting
- **Vector Normalization**: $L_2$ Normalization ($\|v\|_2 = 1.0$) applied to all output vectors
- **Similarity Metric**: Cosine Distance ($1 - \text{Cosine Similarity} = 1 - \langle u, v \rangle$)
