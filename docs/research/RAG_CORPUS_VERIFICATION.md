## 1. Corpus Version History & Reconciliation

During early research iterations, documentation referred to different chunk counts:
- **14 Chunks:** Initial Proof-of-Concept baseline (`index_baseline_v1_14chunks`). Contained only selected primary sections of the DPDP Act 2023 (Sections 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14).
- **70 Chunks:** Intermediate complete extraction of the primary statutory articles of the DPDP Act 2023 and the core rules of the DPDP Rules 2025.
- **83 Chunks:** Authoritative Production Corpus (`backend/knowledge/index/trustforge.faiss`). Formed by combining the 70 statutory text chunks with the 13 Schedule chunks of the notified DPDP Rules 2025 (covering Consent Manager technical specifications, First Schedule criteria, and breach notification formats).

Both the baseline (14 chunks) and the authoritative corpus (83 chunks) are preserved on disk for reproducible comparative experiments:
- Baseline V1: `backend/knowledge/index_baseline_v1_14chunks/`
- Authoritative V2: `backend/knowledge/index/`
